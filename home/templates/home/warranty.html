Warranty Policy

At Camura, we want you to feel confident about your CCTV security products and installation services. Read this policy to understand warranty coverage, exclusions, and how to request assistance.

1. Product Warranty

Warranty coverage depends on the product, manufacturer, model, and applicable warranty terms. The periods below are general guidance only and are not a guarantee for every product sold by Camura.

Product Indicative Warranty Important Note
CCTV Cameras 1–2 years As specified by the manufacturer.

Please note: Always check the invoice, product warranty card, or manufacturer's warranty terms for the exact coverage period. The actual warranty may be shorter or longer than the indicative periods shown above.

2. Installation Warranty

Camura provides a 30-day workmanship warranty for eligible CCTV installation work carried out by our technicians.

  • Coverage applies to eligible issues directly caused by installation workmanship.
  • Customers should contact us and explain the problem so we can assess it.
  • Product defects are handled according to the applicable manufacturer warranty.
  • Additional work, replacement parts, or repairs outside the coverage may incur charges after inspection and approval.

3. What Is Not Covered?

Unless the applicable manufacturer warranty or service agreement states otherwise, the following issues are generally excluded from installation workmanship coverage:

  • Physical damage: Broken cameras, damaged connectors, cracked housings, or impact damage.
  • Wire cuts or tampering: Cables disconnected, cut, or altered after installation.
  • Rodent or pest damage: Cables or equipment damaged by rats or other animals.
  • Electrical surges: Damage caused by voltage fluctuations, lightning, or electrical faults.
  • Unauthorized repairs: Damage caused by unauthorized opening, modification, or repair.
  • Environmental damage: Water ingress, fire, corrosion, or other external causes not covered by the applicable warranty.
  • Third-party changes: Changes to wiring, networking, routers, or equipment made by others that cause the problem.

These exclusions do not override any mandatory rights or protections available under applicable law.

4. How to Request Warranty Service

Follow these steps to help us assess your warranty request:

  1. Keep your purchase invoice and warranty card, if supplied.
  2. Contact Camura and explain the fault or installation issue.
  3. Share the product model, serial number, and photos or videos of the issue when requested.
  4. Allow us to assess whether the issue falls under product warranty or installation workmanship coverage.
  5. If manufacturer service is required, we will guide you on the applicable process, subject to the manufacturer's terms.
Do not open or attempt to repair equipment under warranty before checking the manufacturer's terms. Unauthorized repairs may affect warranty eligibility.

5. Important Warranty Conditions

  • Warranty periods and coverage are determined by the applicable invoice, manufacturer policy, and service agreement.
  • Keep your invoice and product serial numbers for reference.
  • Inspection may be required before warranty eligibility can be confirmed.
  • Warranty does not automatically include relocation, rewiring, upgrades, or unrelated maintenance.
  • Any applicable inspection, transport, or out-of-warranty service charges will be explained before chargeable work proceeds.
  • Camura's installation workmanship warranty is separate from the manufacturer's product warranty.
