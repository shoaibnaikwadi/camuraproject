Which CCTV System Do You Need?

HD CCTV Cameras

Often suitable when upgrading or installing a conventional wired DVR-based surveillance system. Compare resolution, night vision and lens coverage.

IP CCTV Cameras

Network cameras can support higher resolutions and flexible NVR-based installations. Network design and storage capacity matter.

Wi-Fi Smart Cameras

A practical option for selected indoor or small-area uses where reliable Wi-Fi and suitable power are available. Features vary by model.

How Our Service Booking Works

Book an inspection for an existing CCTV issue or contact us about a new installation. Inspection and repair costs are separate.

  1. Submit a request. Sign in and describe your CCTV issue, preferred visit date and Dharashiv city address.
  2. Inspection. We check the system and identify possible repairs or replacement parts.
  3. Review the estimate. Any additional repair, parts or installation charges are explained after inspection and require your approval.

The amount paid during service booking covers inspection only, not additional repairs or parts. Visit our Terms & Conditions for details.
