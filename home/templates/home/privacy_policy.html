Privacy commitment: Camura aims to handle personal information responsibly and use it only for legitimate business purposes, providing services and meeting applicable legal requirements. Please read this policy to understand our practices.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Camura (N S Corporation), operating through camura.in, and explains how we handle information relating to website visitors, customers and people who contact us.

Camura provides CCTV products and related installation, configuration, repair and technical support services. Our on-site service area is limited to Dharashiv city.

By using our website or submitting information to us, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to read this policy. Where applicable law requires consent, we will seek consent as required by that law.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website and services, we may collect:

  • Contact details: name, mobile number, email address and other details you provide.
  • Order information: products purchased, quantities, order history, billing details and delivery address.
  • Service information: installation address, service requests, reported technical issues, appointment details and relevant communications.
  • Account information: account identifiers, profile details and information needed to manage your account.
  • Payment references: transaction identifiers, payment status, refund information and related records.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device information, access logs and website usage data, where collected by our systems or service providers.
  • Communication information: messages, feedback, enquiries and information you voluntarily provide to customer support.
  • Referral information: referral codes and qualifying order information needed to administer an affiliate programme, where applicable.

Please provide only information reasonably necessary for your enquiry, order or service request.

3. How We Collect Information

Information may be collected when you:

  • Create or update an account.
  • Place an order or make a payment.
  • Submit a service booking or contact form.
  • Contact us by telephone, email or WhatsApp.
  • Participate in a referral programme.
  • Browse our website, where analytics or technical logs are enabled.

We may also receive limited transaction or delivery information from payment providers, delivery partners and other service providers involved in fulfilling your request.

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use relevant information to:

  • Process orders, deliveries, cancellations and refunds.
  • Respond to questions and provide customer support.
  • Arrange and manage eligible CCTV service bookings.
  • Verify accounts and deliver OTPs when applicable.
  • Provide order updates and important service notifications.
  • Maintain records for accounting, taxation and legal compliance.
  • Detect suspicious activity, prevent fraud and protect our website.
  • Understand website performance and improve our services.
  • Administer referral commissions and resolve related disputes.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations or respond to lawful requests.

We do not intend to use personal information for purposes incompatible with the reason it was collected without an appropriate legal basis.

5. Orders, Payments & Refunds

When you purchase CCTV products, we use relevant information to process the order, confirm payment, arrange delivery and provide after-sales support.

Online payments may be handled by third-party payment gateways. Such providers may process transaction information under their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

  • We may retain order numbers, payment references, payment status and refund records.
  • Your bank or payment provider may independently retain information about your transaction.
  • We do not intend to store complete card credentials, CVV numbers or payment passwords on our website.
  • Cash-on-delivery orders may require contact and delivery information to fulfil the purchase.

Payment processing, cancellations, delivery and refund eligibility are also subject to our applicable terms and policies.

6. Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential website functions, maintain shopping sessions, improve security and understand website performance.

If Google Analytics or similar analytics tools are enabled, they may process technical and usage information according to their settings and applicable privacy terms.

  • Essential cookies may be needed for login, cart and session functionality.
  • Analytics technologies may help us understand website visits and performance.
  • You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings.

Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features. Where consent is legally required for non-essential technologies, we will handle them according to applicable requirements.

7. OTP Verification & Account Security

If mobile OTP login or registration is available, your mobile number may be used to send verification codes and help protect access to your account.

  • Never share your OTP with another person.
  • We may use verification and security logs to investigate suspicious activity.
  • SMS delivery may involve third-party messaging providers.
  • We may retain relevant authentication records for security and operational purposes.

Camura staff should not need your OTP, account password or payment PIN to provide ordinary customer support.

8. CCTV Installation & Service Privacy

To provide CCTV installation, configuration, repair or maintenance, we may need your contact details, service address, equipment information and a description of the issue.

Customers are responsible for ensuring they have appropriate authority to install and operate CCTV equipment at their premises and to record people where legally permitted.

  • Customers should not share CCTV passwords, administrator credentials or OTPs through ordinary contact forms.
  • Access to devices or recordings, if needed for troubleshooting, should be limited to what is necessary and appropriately authorised.
  • Customers should change default device passwords and control who can access their cameras and recordings.
  • Any customer footage or recordings that we may access during a service should be handled responsibly and in accordance with applicable law and the agreed service scope.
Important: CCTV footage is not automatically collected by Camura merely because you purchase a camera. Any access to recordings depends on the actual service provided and the circumstances involved.

9. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell customers' personal information to third parties. We may share information where reasonably necessary with:

  • Delivery partners: to deliver products and manage delivery issues.
  • Payment providers: to process transactions, refunds and payment verification.
  • Messaging providers: to send OTPs and transactional messages, where enabled.
  • Technical service providers: to host, maintain, secure or operate our website.
  • Service personnel: to arrange authorised installation, repairs or technical support where necessary.
  • Professional advisers: where reasonably needed for legal, accounting or compliance purposes.
  • Authorities: where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Information shared with service providers should be limited to what is appropriate for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable legal requirements and the arrangements in place with those providers.

10. Affiliate & Referral Programme

If you participate in the Camura affiliate or referral programme, we may process your referral code, qualifying order information and commission records to attribute eligible purchases and administer payouts.

Referral links may contain an identifier that helps associate a visit or order with a referring participant. Customers can contact us if they have questions about how a referral is attributed.

Affiliate access, commission eligibility, approval, reversals and payouts are governed by the applicable programme terms. Participation does not necessarily guarantee a commission.

11. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

This may include retaining information to:

  • Fulfil orders, warranties and service commitments.
  • Maintain tax, accounting and transaction records.
  • Resolve complaints, disputes, chargebacks or fraud investigations.
  • Meet legal obligations and establish or defend legal claims.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete it, anonymise it or otherwise handle it according to applicable law and our operational capabilities.

You can contact us to request deletion of eligible personal information. Some records may need to be retained where the law requires it or a valid exception applies.

12. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

No website, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will assess the circumstances and take steps required by applicable law, including any required notices to affected individuals or authorities.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information associated with you. You may also have rights relating to consent, grievances and the handling of your information.

To make a request, contact us using the details provided below. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

  • Ask us to correct inaccurate contact details.
  • Request information about how your personal data is handled.
  • Request deletion of eligible information, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Raise a concern or grievance about our handling of personal information.

We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law. Certain requests may be restricted where a legal obligation or permitted exception applies.

14. Children's Privacy

Our website and CCTV shopping services are not specifically directed at children. Individuals should not submit another person's personal information without appropriate authority.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in circumstances where it should not have been collected, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate steps.

15. Third-Party Websites & Services

Our website may link to third-party services, including payment providers, maps, messaging platforms and external websites.

These services may have their own privacy policies, cookie practices and terms. Camura does not control the independent privacy practices of third parties. Review their policies before providing information directly to them.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology, business practices or legal obligations change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for relevant changes.
