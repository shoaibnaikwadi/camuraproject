Shipping & Delivery Policy

1. Delivery Locations

Camura offers local delivery in Dharashiv and delivery to other locations across India where shipping service is available for the product and destination.

  • Dharashiv: Local delivery availability depends on the delivery address, stock and arrangements.
  • Other locations in India: Delivery depends on courier coverage, product availability and serviceability of the destination PIN code.
Important service-area restriction: On-site CCTV installation, repair and technical services are available in Dharashiv city only. Product delivery outside Dharashiv does not mean that on-site installation is available at that location.

2. Estimated Delivery Time

The following are indicative estimates from our existing delivery policy. Actual delivery times should be confirmed against your shipping method and order details.

Local Delivery

1–2 days

Indicative estimate for eligible local deliveries in Dharashiv, subject to stock and scheduling.

Pan-India Delivery

3–7 days

Indicative estimate for serviceable destinations across India, subject to courier coverage and dispatch.

Delivery estimates may be affected by weekends, public holidays, remote destinations, courier capacity, weather, stock availability or other events beyond our reasonable control.

Unless expressly confirmed otherwise, these estimates are not guaranteed delivery dates.

3. Order Processing & Dispatch

After you place an order, we may review the order, confirm product availability, verify payment status where applicable and prepare the items for dispatch.

  • Orders are processed according to product availability and the applicable payment method.
  • Orders requiring payment confirmation may not be dispatched until the payment is verified.
  • We may contact you to clarify an incomplete address or other delivery information.
  • If a product becomes unavailable or a material issue affects fulfilment, we will contact you about the available options.

An order confirmation does not necessarily mean that the parcel has already been dispatched. Dispatch status should be checked through any order updates provided by Camura.

4. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, may depend on the delivery address, package size, product weight, courier service and order value.

  • Any shipping fee shown during checkout or separately communicated for your order will apply to that order.
  • Additional charges will be communicated before they are collected.
  • Installation, configuration, inspection and repair charges are separate from product shipping charges unless explicitly stated otherwise.

If shipping charges are not displayed online, please contact us to confirm the applicable delivery cost before placing the order.

5. Shipment Tracking & Updates

Where tracking is available, we may provide shipment details or order-status updates through the contact information associated with your order.

  • Keep your registered mobile number and email address accessible.
  • Check any courier tracking link supplied for your order.
  • Contact us if the tracking information appears incorrect or has not updated for an extended period.

Tracking information may be delayed while the courier updates its systems. Actual parcel movement is handled by the relevant delivery provider.

6. Delivery Requirements

To help ensure successful delivery, please:

  • Provide a complete and accurate delivery address and PIN code.
  • Provide a reachable mobile number for delivery coordination.
  • Ensure someone authorised is available to receive the parcel.
  • Check the parcel packaging and product count at delivery, where possible.
  • Retain the invoice, packaging and order details in case support is needed.

Please do not share OTPs, payment PINs or account passwords with anyone claiming to be a courier or Camura representative unless you have verified the request through an appropriate official process.

7. Damaged, Missing or Incorrect Items

If a product arrives damaged, an item is missing or you receive an incorrect product, contact Camura as soon as reasonably possible.

  • Provide your order number and a description of the issue.
  • Share clear photographs of the parcel, packaging and product if available.
  • Retain the original packaging and invoice until the issue is resolved.

We will review the circumstances and explain the available resolution under the applicable return, replacement, refund and warranty terms.

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude consumer rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.

8. Installation & Service Scheduling

Purchasing a CCTV product does not automatically include installation unless installation is expressly included in the product listing, order confirmation or a separate agreement.

  • Installation is scheduled after product delivery or availability is confirmed, as applicable.
  • Appointment timing depends on customer availability, technician availability and the nature of the work.
  • Installation or repair charges will depend on the agreed service scope and applicable charges communicated to the customer.
  • The customer should arrange safe access to the installation location and disclose relevant site restrictions.
Dharashiv city only: Camura's on-site installation and repair services are limited to Dharashiv city. Confirm service availability before booking an installation or repair appointment.

9. Failed or Undeliverable Shipments

A delivery may fail because of an incorrect address, an unavailable recipient, restricted access or courier-related circumstances.

If a parcel cannot be delivered, we may contact you to arrange a suitable next step, subject to the courier's procedures and the circumstances of the order.

Any additional delivery cost, cancellation, refund or reshipment will be handled under the applicable order terms and consumer law. We will communicate relevant charges before collecting them.

10. Delivery Delays & Exceptions

Delivery may be delayed by circumstances such as:

  • Unexpected stock shortages or supplier delays.
  • Severe weather, transport disruption or other emergencies.
  • Courier capacity limitations or service interruptions.
  • Incorrect or incomplete delivery details.
  • Remote delivery locations or other logistical constraints.

Where we become aware of a significant issue affecting your order, we will make reasonable efforts to communicate relevant updates and available options.

Statutory consumer rights and any applicable cancellation or refund obligations remain unaffected.
