Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy

We want you to shop with confidence at Camura. Please read our policy to understand return eligibility, refund processing, order cancellation, and CCTV installation service cancellations.

1. Return Eligibility

Return requests may be made within 7 days of delivery, subject to inspection and the conditions below.

  • The product must generally be unused and in its original packaging, with supplied accessories and documentation.
  • Eligible manufacturing defects may qualify for a return, replacement, repair, or other remedy after assessment.
  • Customers should provide the order number, invoice, and a description or photos of the issue.
  • Return approval is subject to product condition, applicable manufacturer terms, and applicable law.
Please contact Camura before sending any product back. Returns sent without prior instructions may take longer to assess.

2. Non-returnable Products

The following products may not be eligible for a change-of-mind return, subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the case:

  • Installed CCTV cameras: Products already installed or used, except where a valid defect or other legally applicable remedy applies.
  • Opened DVR/NVR systems: Opened, configured, or used equipment, except for eligible defects or other applicable remedies.
  • Wires and accessories: Cut cables, used wiring, or accessories that cannot reasonably be resold in their original condition.
  • Physically damaged products: Items damaged after delivery due to mishandling, unauthorized modification, or other causes not attributable to the seller.
  • Specially configured products: Items configured or supplied specifically to a customer's requirements, where return restrictions are permitted by law.

These restrictions do not exclude remedies available for manufacturing defects, incorrect products, transit damage, or other circumstances where a remedy is required by law.

3. Refund Process & Timeline

Once an approved return is received and inspected, we will inform you of the outcome and any applicable refund or replacement arrangement.

  1. Contact our support team and request a return or refund.
  2. Provide your order details, invoice, and relevant evidence of the issue.
  3. Follow the return instructions and submit the product for inspection, where required.
  4. After approval, the refund will generally be processed within 5–7 business days.
  5. The time taken for the amount to appear in your account may depend on your bank, card issuer, or payment provider.
The 5–7 business day period refers to our intended processing time after inspection and approval. It does not necessarily include shipping, inspection, or bank processing time.

4. Order Cancellation Policy

Customers may request cancellation before an order is shipped. Cancellation is subject to the order's current processing status.

  • Before shipment: Contact us as soon as possible to request cancellation.
  • Already shipped: Cancellation may no longer be possible. Please contact us for instructions on the available options.
  • Prepaid orders: If cancellation is approved, any applicable refund will be processed using the original payment method where possible.
  • Delivered orders: Return requests are assessed under the return policy rather than the pre-shipment cancellation policy.

Submitting a cancellation request does not mean it has been approved. Please wait for confirmation from Camura.

5. CCTV Installation Cancellation

If you need to cancel or reschedule a CCTV installation or service appointment, please contact us as early as possible.

  • Before technician dispatch: Contact us to request cancellation or rescheduling.
  • After technician dispatch: A reasonable, disclosed cancellation or visit charge may apply where permitted, based on the service arrangements and costs incurred.
  • After work begins: Charges may apply for work already completed, materials used, or other costs previously communicated and agreed to.
  • Service inspection charges: Any inspection charge communicated at the time of booking will be governed by the terms shown before payment.
We will explain any applicable cancellation charges before collecting them. Charges will not override any rights or remedies available under applicable law.

6. Damaged, Incorrect or Defective Products

If your order arrives damaged, you receive an incorrect item, or a product appears to have a manufacturing defect, please contact us promptly.

  • Keep the product, packaging, invoice, and accessories until the issue is reviewed.
  • Share clear photos or videos of the product and any visible damage, where possible.
  • Do not discard packaging or attempt repairs before receiving instructions, if practical.
  • We will assess the issue and explain the available remedy, which may include repair, replacement, return, or refund as appropriate.
