Who We Are

Camura is a Dharashiv-based CCTV and security solutions business operated by N S Corporation. We help homeowners, shopkeepers, offices and other businesses choose and maintain suitable surveillance systems.

Whether you need a new CCTV setup, help configuring your DVR or NVR, mobile viewing assistance, or repair of an existing system, our focus is on practical solutions and dependable technical support.

  • CCTV camera and surveillance product guidance
  • CCTV installation and DVR/NVR configuration
  • Mobile viewing setup and technical troubleshooting
  • Repair and maintenance support for existing systems
Our Service Area: Dharashiv City Only

Our on-site CCTV service and repair support is limited to Dharashiv city. Please contact us to confirm service availability before booking.
